## Files and behavior

- Scheduling: optional catalog `required_resources` (unique role/kind pairs, primary first),
  `slots.py` joint availability, `holds.py` allocation, `resources.py` CRUD and `reads.py` lifecycle
  reads/jobs. Existing service/public entry points remain compatible. Appointments keep their primary
  and expose provider/chair names. The ledger is exported with the module.
- Database: `appointment_resources` has tenant/composite FKs, RLS, a resource/time exclusion constraint
  and parent synchronization triggers. Every new/updated primary is mirrored into the same constraint
  domain as secondary allocations, including single-resource services. No migration backfill rows;
  legacy primary bookings are also checked by the ledger trigger. Expiry/cancel/completion and reschedule
  update all occupied ranges. Booking confirmation rechecks current requirements and may reallocate
  secondary resources. Staff hours override does not bypass multi-resource availability.
- Conversations/AI: enabled locale keywords synchronously hand over inbound visitor/customer messages.
  Safety v3 adds an explicit emergency signal; enabled clinics require this classification before tools.
  Missing/unavailable classification escalates conservatively. Intake v7 also recognizes emergencies.
  C1 pauses ownership, invalidates reply drafts, sends deterministic clinic instructions plus the local
  emergency number sentence, and notifies staff. Work items are urgent and paginated ahead of ordinary
  work. AI booking execution rechecks conversation ownership before any writes.
- Org: default-off emergency handling/instructions; seed-only `synthetic_demo`. Backend-owned exact
  legal text is returned with business/settings/public-profile reads. API lifespan checks a restricted,
  boolean-only security-definer function across tenant RLS. Regulated seed refuses before reset/writes.
- Seed/portable fixtures: three businesses; 15 invented dental patients, dentist/hygienist/front desk,
  two providers, three chairs, four treatments, supplies/reorder rules and supplier, four published FAQ
  articles, terminology/minimal templates, shadow autonomy and enabled Customer Care. Demo logins discover
  staff from fixtures through the existing command. Dental appointment history is relative to seed time.
- Frontend: existing service create/edit forms expose requirements and recall interval; policy settings
  expose emergency handling/instructions; slot/detail/grid show all allocations. Backend banners appear
  in the application shell (Inbox, AI Team, Autonomy, Knowledge), inside the assistant and in public chat.
  Labels use i18n; the required legal statement remains exactly the backend text.
- Documentation: P10-E1 contracts/M06 first, module cards, README AI section, dental demo-script section,
  fixture validation and six new intake eval cases (five emergencies, one normal-visit boundary).

## Decisions and boundaries

D5=a remains the owner's decision: standard hosted Vultr routes, synthetic data only, visible banner.
No inference-provider/routing configuration was changed. Real PHI requires clinic-controlled local models
under the regulated profile, plus policies, agreements and legal review. No clinical features were added.

One resource per declared kind (staff/room/equipment, at most three). Per-kind service links are strict
allowlists when declared; absent links permit active resources of that kind at that location. Existing
services without requirements preserve their qualification behavior. Room/provider availability includes
all duration and post-service buffer minutes. Multi-location queries use scoped location readers.

Pain, swelling, bleeding, trauma/injury, infection, breathing difficulty, urgent/emergency language or
uncertainty escalate. Keywords deliberately do not infer negation: "not an emergency" also escalates.
English is the fallback; Spanish/Chinese input is recognized too. A rough filling without any urgency or
other trigger may book an ordinary visit after an explicit clean classifier verdict. No medical advice.

Recall uses B2's existing discovery, `customer_care.send_message`, consent, dedupe, future booking and
shadow rules unchanged. Minimal dental messages omit treatment names. Eligible seeded examples: Avery,
Blair, Emery; Cedar has a future booking, Drew lacks consent, Finley/Gray are recent.
