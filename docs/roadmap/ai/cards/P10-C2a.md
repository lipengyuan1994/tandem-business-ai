## Implementation and decisions

- OpenAI-compatible embed capability: explicit 1024 dimensions, batches <=32, finite nonzero float32 vectors,
  route fallbacks/deadlines, egress guard, per-attempt policy/reservations, business and B1 admission/budgets.
  Queries charge the parent AI run; independent index/query calls record metadata only. Regulated embed routes
  require verified private local addresses. Dev embeddings are deterministic hashes, not live quality evidence.
- The composition registry supplies knowledge's embedding provider, preserving the acyclic module graph.
  No TypeSafe service was added: the requested embedding stack is fixed and external calls are prohibited here.
- `kb_chunks`: forced tenant RLS, composite tenant/version FKs, exact spans and 400-word chunks/60-word overlap,
  vector(1024), HNSW cosine index. Materialized tenant/public/published candidates rank exactly before RRF (k=60),
  avoiding global approximate-index filtering losses; HNSW is available for future partitioning.
- One article per durable `ai.knowledge_index` job (<=32 chunks), using the AI worker lane. Model/publication/
  generation are rechecked after inference. Publish queues indexing; new publication, unpublish/archive/private
  visibility remove old chunks immediately. Deletion cascades; erasure invalidates derived data with a generation
  fence. Hourly model checks and write-authorized `/knowledge/reindex` sweeps use 25-article batches/continuations.
- Retrieval reads publication/visibility after embedding, including FTS fallback, to avoid stale lexical hits
  during inference. Citations retain article/version/passages and exact spans; grounding/tool authorization
  stay unchanged. Export includes chunk metadata without copied chunk text or vectors. Re-ranking is deferred.
- Multipart `/knowledge/imports`: one PDF, MIME + `%PDF-`, 10 MiB /100 pages /300,000 extracted characters.
  No uploaded bytes are stored. Resource-limited subprocess parsing creates normalized unpublished drafts with
  sanitized filename/page ranges. Explicit publication remains required; no OCR or object storage.
- Knowledge UI has translated PDF picker/limits/progress/errors/review links and search-index badges. Generated
  OpenAPI/TypeScript schemas were refreshed. Owner-prepared pypdf and lockfile changes were preserved.
